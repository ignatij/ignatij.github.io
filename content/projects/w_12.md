## The problem

W12 is an internal ERP system that needed to display different PostgreSQL datasets through a generic interface while supporting the company's operational and financial-reporting work.

## My contribution

I led the team responsible for active development and maintenance, acted as the primary contact for client requirements, and stayed hands-on across the system. My work included Go and Java services, PostgreSQL, the Svelte frontend, CI/CD pipelines, and Kubernetes deployments.

I also worked directly with the company's controllers, which required understanding income statements, balance sheets, and accruals rather than treating reporting as a purely technical export.

## Engineering decisions

- Used a dedicated Go service to provide generic views over PostgreSQL data.
- Kept other business logic in Java services within the microservice system.
- Delivered the user interface with Svelte and deployed the services on Kubernetes.

## Supported result

The system generated monthly financial statements directly from its data, supporting delivery within **one to five days after month-end** and reducing the risk of discrepancies from manually reconstructed reports.
