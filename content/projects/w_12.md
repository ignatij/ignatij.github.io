## Context & risk

W12 is an internal ERP system responsible for exposing diverse PostgreSQL datasets through generic views while driving month-end financial reporting. When financial statements are reconstructed manually outside the source of record, reporting slows down and reconciliation discrepancies become hard to audit.

## Architecture & key tradeoffs

- **Dedicated Go view service alongside Java domain services:** Kept core business logic in existing **Java** microservices while using a dedicated **Go** service to render generic views over **PostgreSQL** datasets without duplicating schema-specific boilerplate across every reporting screen.
- **Domain-accurate financial workflows:** Worked directly with company controllers on income statements, balance sheets, and accruals so accounting semantics lived in the system rather than in ad-hoc spreadsheet exports.
- **Delivery:** Paired a **Svelte** UI with containerized services deployed through CI/CD onto **Kubernetes**.

## Role & outcome

- Led the development and maintenance team and served as the primary technical contact for client requirements and delivery.
- Supported system-derived monthly financial statements delivered within **1–5 days after month-end**, reducing discrepancy risk from manual report reconstruction.
